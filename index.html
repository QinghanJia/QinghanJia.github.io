Publications

ContactWAM: From Handheld Force–Tactile Demonstrations to Contact-Predictive World Action Model

Qinghan Jia, et al.

Under review at ICRA 2027

Project Page

A world action model whose contact expert predicts future touch and wrench together with video and actions. It reaches 77.5% mean success on four real-robot contact-rich tasks, compared with 43.8% for π0.5 and 45.0% for vision-only FastWAM.

Demo2Arm: From Human Demonstrations to Task-Specific Robotic Arms via Differentiable Co-Design

Qinghan Jia, et al.

Under review at ICRA 2027

Project Page

A differentiable co-design framework that turns demonstrated trajectories and six-axis wrenches into task-specific arm designs, cutting link deflection by 17.2% and peak torque utilization by 16.2%.
